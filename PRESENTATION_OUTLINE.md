# Presentation (7 slides)

## Slide 1 — Title
A Digital QR-Based Menu & Food Ordering System  
Student name, roll number, class, guide.

## Slide 2 — Introduction
A web-based system that lets customers view a restaurant menu and place orders through a digital menu opened using a QR code.

## Slide 3 — Problem and Objectives
Problem: printed menus may become outdated and manual order recording takes time.  
Objectives: digital menu, cart, automatic total, order database, admin order management.

## Slide 4 — Technologies
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, XAMPP, GitHub.

## Slide 5 — System Modules
Customer: menu, quantity, cart, place order.  
Admin: login, manage menu availability, view orders, update status.

## Slide 6 — Testing and Demonstration
Show menu → add item → place order → admin login → update order status. Include actual screenshots and actual test results.

## Slide 7 — Conclusion and Future Scope
The prototype demonstrates a simple digital ordering workflow. Future scope: payment gateway, live tracking, table-specific QR codes and analytics.
